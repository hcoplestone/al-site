Vision

To build a global cultural adventure tour operator which leads the travel industry towards a more sustainable future and protects travel for future generations.

BHAG  ITG will become the world's number one specialist travel company — measuring success on the triple bottom line of People, Planet, and Profit. Positive impact matters as much as financial results. Everything we do should deliver value for our customers, our staff, our suppliers, our destinations, the communities we operate within, and our shareholders. We’ll build our success through Cultural Adventure and destination specialist brands, but we won’t stop there. Inside Travel will stand for specialism - not one specific type of specialism. And we’ll deliver it all through the ITG formula for success: Customer Obsession + Specialism + Business for Good.
Mission

Our Mission: To make our clients happy, to be a place to work that our teams recommend and to have a positive impact on our destinations and local communities.

Our niche

Our Niche: Expertly designed, seamlessly delivered Cultural Adventure trips for clients who value caring, personalized service at every interaction.
